# Security policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made for the latest published minor version of
`@lightleaplabs/ui` and `@lightleaplabs/tokens`.

## Report a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

Email [support@lightleaplabs.com](mailto:support@lightleaplabs.com) with the
subject `[SECURITY] Vulnerability report`. Include:

- the affected package and version;
- impact and the conditions required to reproduce it;
- a minimal reproduction or proof of concept;
- any suggested mitigation; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

We will acknowledge receipt within three business days and provide an initial
assessment within seven business days. Please allow us a reasonable period to
investigate and publish a fix before disclosing the report publicly.
